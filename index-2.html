Watch the ad. Pick the best price.

Local businesses post their posters and videos with prices side by side. Tap an offer to go straight to the seller.

For shoppers

  1. Browse poster and video ads on Home and Videos.
  2. Use the search bar to find a shop or product.
  3. Compare the prices listed for each ad. The cheapest is marked.
  4. Tap the offer button to go straight to the seller.

For business owners

  1. Tap Post your ad and pay by scanning the UPI QR.
  2. Enter the UPI reference number from your receipt.
  3. Add your headline, format, two or three seller prices, and your link.
  4. Publish. Your ad appears at the top of Home.
