For shoppers

  1. Browse poster and video ads on Home and Videos.
  2. Search by typing, or tap the microphone and speak.
  3. Compare the prices. The cheapest is marked.
  4. Tap the offer button to go straight to the seller.

For business owners

  1. Tap Post your ad and fill in your details.
  2. Make a poster in the Poster studio, or let Promo make one.
  3. Pay securely on Razorpay (UPI, cards and netbanking).
  4. Send your ad details. It goes live once your payment is confirmed.
